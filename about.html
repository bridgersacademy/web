Our story

Bridging a gap that matters.

Young people learn in many places: in classrooms, through stories, from conversations, by observing others, and through experience. Yet there can still be a gap between what they are taught and what they need to navigate everyday life.

Bridgers Academy seeks to help bridge that gap by making relevant knowledge accessible through publishing, educational content, training, speaking engagements, and community activities.

Our tagline

“Bridging the gap between young ones and knowledge.”

Our work centres on connecting learning with the questions, choices, and experiences young people encounter as they grow.
